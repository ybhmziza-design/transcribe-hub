Transcription Style Guide & Handbook

Official formatting and quality standards for transcribers on TranscribeHub.

📖 1. Clean Verbatim vs. Strict Verbatim

Unless specified otherwise in the client's guidelines, our default format across the platform is Clean Verbatim.

✓ Clean Verbatim (Default)
  • Remove filler words: "um", "uh", "ah", "er"
  • Remove false starts and stuttering (e.g., "I th- I think" → "I think")
  • Preserve slang like "gonna", "wanna" only if essential to tone; prefer formal spelling
  • Maintain speaker meaning without editorial rewriting
★ Strict Verbatim (When Requested)
  • Capture every vocalization, hesitation, and repetition
  • Keep all stutters: "I, um, I was going to, ah, mention..."
  • Include throat clearing and verbal tics
  • Required for legal depositions and linguistics studies

🗣️ 2. Speaker Labels & Tags

Clearly separate dialogue whenever speakers change.

Interviewer: Welcome to the conference. Can you explain your architecture?

Dr. Sterling: Absolutely. We transitioned to a distributed ledger model last quarter.

Interviewer: How did that impact transaction latency?

⏱️ 3. Timestamps & Non-Speech Events

Use standard bracket tags for sound events and speech interruptions:

[inaudible 02:15] When a word or phrase cannot be deciphered after 3 listens, add timestamp inside tag.
[crosstalk] When two or more individuals talk simultaneously over each other.
[laughter] / [applause] Significant crowd reactions or prolonged laughter that pauses the speaker.
[music] Intro/outro theme tracks or background audio interludes.
